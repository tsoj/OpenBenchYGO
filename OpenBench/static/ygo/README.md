# Theme assets (ygo.css)

| Files | Source |
|---|---|
| `cloud-*.jpg`, `parch-*.jpg` | Seamless textures made for this theme: periodic noise (FFT) whose colors are matched to the frame and text box pixels of the card templates of [kooriookami/yugioh-card](https://github.com/kooriookami/yugioh-card) (MIT). 512px tiles; `*-256.jpg`: the same shrunk to 256px (the CSS draws each tile at its own size) |
| `cloud-dsynchro.jpg`, `dparch-dsynchro.jpg` | The same, colors matched to the frame and text box of Dark Synchro cards |
| `effectbox.png` | The text box frame, cut from those templates (pixels equal in the Effect, Spell, Ritual and Trap templates) at 0.75 of their size; its shadows as transparency |
| `attr-dark.png`, `attr-light.png` | Attribute icons of kooriookami/yugioh-card |
| `ygo-en-name.woff2`, `ygo-en.woff2`, `ygo-en-race.woff2` | Card fonts of kooriookami/yugioh-card (name, text, type line) |
| `hiero.jpg`, `hiero-dark.jpg` | Columns of the Papyrus of Ani ([Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Papyrus_ani_curs_hiero.jpg), public domain), with a glyph made from a Black Rose Dragon picture |

Card frame designs, fonts and card art belong to their owners (Konami, the fonts' foundries, the artists).
